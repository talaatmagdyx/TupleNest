# TupleNest brand

The name is always **TupleNest**: one word, with a capital T and N. In the
logo, "Tuple" is navy and "Nest" is blue.

## The mark

A hexagon drawn in two strokes: bright blue at the top left and navy at the
bottom right, with a gap where they meet. Inside is a small table, with a
darker header row over two light rows. It stands for rows held safely.

- **Clear space:** at least a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
- **Smallest size:** 16 px for the mark and icon, 96 px wide for the full
  logo.
- **On dark backgrounds,** use the dark logo. Its navy becomes `#DCE6FA`.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | `#1A6DFF` | The mark's top stroke, "Nest", links and badges |
| Navy | `#0B1F5C` | The mark's bottom stroke, "Tuple", headings on light backgrounds |
| Header blue | `#1A5FD0` | The table's header row; the app's accent in the light theme |
| Cell blue | `#B9CDF2` | The table's body rows |
| Dark navy replacement | `#DCE6FA` | The navy parts, on dark backgrounds |
| App accent, dark theme | `#5C9DFF` | Buttons and focus in the dark theme |

Colours are flat: no gradients, glows or drop shadows on the logo.

## Type

- **Space Grotesk:** headings and the wordmark.
- **Inter:** body text and the app's interface.
- **A monospace font:** code.

Both Space Grotesk and Inter are under the SIL Open Font License, and copies
are in `docs/site/fonts`.

## Don't

- Recolour, stretch, rotate or outline the logo.
- Put the light logo on a dark background, or the reverse.
- Add effects such as gradients, shadows or 3D.
- Pair the mark with another name, or use it for a fork as though it were
  official.

## Writing style

- Write plainly, as an engineer to other engineers.
- Keep paragraphs short and use precise terms.
- Avoid buzzwords and use emoji sparingly.
- Claim only what the code does. Say where a protection stops.
